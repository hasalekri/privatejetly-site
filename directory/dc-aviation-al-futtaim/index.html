DC Aviation Al-Futtaim

Joint venture of Germany's DC Aviation and Dubai's Al-Futtaim. Opened the first FBO at Dubai World Central in 2013, with a 5,700 m² hangar and a 1,300 m² lounge. IS-BAH Stage 3 for handling.
