Glossary

Glossary

Business-aviation terms in plain English, with Arabic equivalents where they are commonly used.

AOC (Air Operator Certificate)شهادة مشغّل جوي
The approval a national aviation authority gives a company to carry passengers or cargo commercially. A charter flight should be operated by a company holding an AOC that covers it.
Block timeزمن الرحلة الكلي
Time from when the aircraft first moves for departure until it stops at the destination. Charter is often billed on block or flight hours, depending on the operator.
Cabotageالنقل الداخلي
Carrying passengers between two points inside one country. Often reserved for that country's own operators.
CAMO
Continuing Airworthiness Management Organisation. It manages the maintenance planning and records that keep an aircraft airworthy.
Empty legرحلة فارغة
A flight with no passengers, usually repositioning an aircraft. Sometimes offered at a discount. JetMajlis does not list empty legs.
FBO (Fixed-Base Operator)مركز خدمة الطائرات الخاصة
A business-aviation facility at an airport offering services such as a passenger lounge, handling, fuelling and parking.
GAT (General Aviation Terminal)
A terminal for non-airline flights, including private and business aircraft.
Great-circle distance
The shortest distance between two points on the Earth's surface. Actual routes are usually longer.
Ground handlingالخدمات الأرضية
Services for an aircraft and its passengers on the ground, such as marshalling, baggage, catering coordination and passenger escort.
ICAO code
A four-letter airport identifier assigned by the International Civil Aviation Organization, for example OBBI for Bahrain International.
IS-BAH
International Standard for Business Aircraft Handling, an industry code of practice for ground handlers, with staged registration.
ktas
Knots true airspeed. The aircraft's speed through the air, in nautical miles per hour.
Mach number (M)
Speed as a fraction of the speed of sound. Mmo is the maximum operating Mach number.
MROالصيانة والإصلاح
Maintenance, repair and overhaul.
Nautical mile (nm)ميل بحري
1,852 metres. Aviation distances and ranges are given in nautical miles.
NBAA IFR reserves
A standard set of fuel-reserve assumptions often used in business-jet range figures.
Positioning
Flying an aircraft to where a trip starts, or back to base after it ends. It often affects the price of a charter.
