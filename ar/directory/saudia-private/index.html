Saudia Private

النسخة العربية من هذه الصفحة قيد الإعداد. يظهر النص الإنجليزي أدناه.

Private-aviation subsidiary of Saudia, founded in 2009 and headquartered in Jeddah. Rebranded from Saudia Private Aviation in October 2023. Provides charter, FBO and aircraft management services.
