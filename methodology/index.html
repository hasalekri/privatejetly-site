Methodology

Methodology

How we collect and verify data, and how the estimator works.

Sources

Aircraft specifications come only from manufacturer websites or official factsheets. If a figure is not in the source we checked, we show “Not listed” and do not fill the gap. Directory listings need at least one public source (the company itself, an official body, or established trade press), and each one shows a last-reviewed date. Airport coordinates come from OurAirports open data. Maps are drawn from Natural Earth (public domain).

What a listing is not

We do not rate safety, award “verified” badges, sell placements, or claim partnerships. We do not publish employees' personal contact details.

The estimator

1. Distance: great-circle (haversine) distance between the two airports, using a mean Earth radius of 3,440.065 nm.

2. Routing: distance × 1.07 to allow for airways, departures and arrivals.

3. Time: routed distance ÷ the class's average block speed, plus 0.3 h per leg for taxi, climb and approach. If a leg exceeds the class's practical range, we add 0.75 h for a fuel stop.

Corrections

We fix errors when we find them and update the review date.
